How do I get my export?
  1. Open the WHOOP app, then More, App Settings, Data Export (Android: More, Data Export).
  2. Choose a date range and tap Export.
  3. Download the .zip from the email WHOOP sends you.
  4. Drop it here. Longer ranges give better findings.

    Compare

    How you stack up

    Your averages against published benchmarks, so you can see where you are ahead and where there is room.

    YouTypical rangeWHOOP member average

    Benchmarks come from WHOOP's published member data (averages for recovery, strain, sleep, HRV and resting heart rate by sex, HRV ranges by age, and typical sleeping breathing rates), plus standard adult guideline ranges for sleep length, efficiency, sleep stages and blood oxygen. WHOOP members tend to be more active and health-conscious than the general public, so treat the average as a reference point, not a target. Calories, sleep consistency and skin temperature have no public benchmark and are not compared. Sources: WHOOP member averages, HRV by age and sex, respiratory rate.
