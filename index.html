    How to get your WHOOP data export
    1. Open the WHOOP app and go to More, then App Settings, then Data Export (on Android: More, then Data Export).
    2. Choose a date range and tap Export.
    3. WHOOP emails you a link. Download the .zip file.
    4. Drop the .zip onto this page. You can also unzip it and drop the CSV files instead.

    Exports are a snapshot, so request a new one whenever you want fresh numbers.
